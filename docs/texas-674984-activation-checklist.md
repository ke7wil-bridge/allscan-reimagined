# Texas 674984 staged activation gates

No later gate implies approval for an earlier or later gate. Record the named
approver, timestamp, reviewed commit, exact host/effect, backup, rollback and
result at every gate. KJ5IRQ/operator decisions remain external blockers.

1. **Operator agreement.** KJ5IRQ confirms ownership, acceptable use, control
   operators, incident contacts, maintenance, retention, evidence sharing,
   provider console, costs, data jurisdiction and credential revocation.
2. **Policy approval.** Approve the proposed 60-inbound policy, local bridge
   exclusions, abuse threshold, permanent-link policy and emergency isolation.
3. **Read-only host preflight.** Confirm Texas OS/ASL3/ASR compatibility, node
   674984 identity, DNS/NTP/network/source IP, resources, firewall and no links.
4. **Backup and credential approval.** Approve snapshot/backup locations and
   creation of unique Texas SSH, HMAC and AMI credentials plus pinned host key.
5. **Telemetry-only installation.** Approve exact Texas file/unit/dialplan
   writes and the exact Kentucky forced/source-restricted key and isolated
   central status registration. No radio link; `controlPolling=false`.
6. **Telemetry acceptance.** Verify identity, freshness, monotonic sequence,
   capacity display 60, stale/offline behavior, audit hygiene and rollback.
7. **Supervised radio link.** Approve one scheduled test link with named test
   nodes; verify both audio directions, loop protection and 60/61 admission.
8. **Supervised disconnect.** Separately enable and test only disconnect, then
   disable it and verify ledger/ack/audit state and unchanged unrelated links.
9. **Incremental controls.** Separately approve connect, then ban, then unban.
   Each permission needs a scoped identity, supervised test and revocation test.
10. **Final acceptance.** KJ5IRQ and Netoholics accept measured behavior,
    remaining risks, incident/rollback ownership and deployed hashes. Only then
    may normal operation be considered; no load-balancing, geographic-routing
    or hardware-capacity claim is implied.

Any identity mismatch, stale/future telemetry, unexplained queue/inflight item,
failed audit, credential exposure, changed link, resource saturation or rollback
failure stops progression. Preserve evidence and use emergency isolation.
