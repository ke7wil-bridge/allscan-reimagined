# AllScan Reimagined v1.0.0 Beta 7.2

Beta 7.2 makes digital Bridge Cards easier to configure and safer to remove.

## What Beta 7.2 adds

- Bridge Cards for DMR, YSF, Zello, P25, NXDN, and M17 now use clearer,
  expandable settings.
- New cards are titled `New Digital Bridge` until the title is changed.
- Digital Mode and Card Title are separate, so the displayed title remains
  freely editable.
- Standard and Net Bridge options, permissions, approved destinations,
  readiness details, advanced settings, and connected-client sources are shown
  only when relevant.
- Deleting a card clearly shows what ASR will remove and what it will leave
  alone. Existing or manually installed bridge systems are not adopted or
  removed.
- Optional startup audio can summarize Standard bridges that are actually
  connected after startup recovery finishes. It is off by default and does not
  announce individual link events.
- Healthy Bridge Cards now show `-` in the Warning / Error row while real
  warnings continue to take priority.
- Additional checks protect Settings saves, bridge ownership records, retries,
  installation, and rollback.
- Active EchoLink connections can keep showing their callsigns when the normal
  EchoLink directory lookup is temporarily unavailable.

## Important bridge note

Beta 7.2 displays and controls compatible bridge systems that were installed
separately. It does not install a new digital bridge system. Managed bridge
installation is planned for a future beta.

## Looking ahead to Beta 8

- A guided way to create new ASR-managed digital bridges.
- Ownership recorded from the beginning, so ASR always knows exactly what it
  created.
- A replacement process when a bridge's mode or role changes.
- Existing manually installed bridges will remain separate and untouched.

Final Beta 8 features may change.
